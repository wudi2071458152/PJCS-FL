Upstream sources and scope

The proxy tensors were optimized from the respective clients' real benchmark
data. Their initialization sampled real examples, and optimization used the
original-loss, 32,000-iteration procedure documented in this repository.
The assets are generated research inputs, not the original benchmark archive.
